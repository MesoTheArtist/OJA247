Start small. Grow big. We handle the rest.

OJA247 helps Nigerian businesses of every size get online and grow. Vendors get a branded storefront with a shareable link, and shoppers can browse and search products from businesses across the marketplace.

For businesses

Open a storefront on the OJA247 marketplace, list your products, and receive order alerts by email and WhatsApp. Brands that need a site of their own can ask about a custom website, with setup and upkeep handled for you.

For shoppers

Explore vendors and shop products from local businesses. Every purchase happens on the vendor's storefront, where you can see their brand and details before you buy.

For marketers

Earn by referring businesses to OJA247. It is free to join, with no inventory and no storefront to manage.
