How buying, paying and receiving works on OJA247, and what to do if something goes wrong.

## How an order works

1. **Choose your items.** Each order is from one store.
2. **Check out.** Enter your details and choose delivery or pickup. You see the total, including the seller's delivery fee, before you place the order.
3. **Pay the seller by bank transfer.** We show you the seller's bank details, the exact amount, and an order reference. Transfer that amount from your bank.
4. **Upload your receipt.** This is required to place the order. Only upload a genuine receipt for a payment you actually made.
5. **The seller confirms.** They check their own bank account and confirm your payment. If something is wrong, they reject it with a reason and you can upload a new receipt.
6. **The seller sends it out.** You get an email, and you can follow your order from your orders page.
7. **You confirm you received it.**

## Paying

- You pay the seller's own bank account directly. OJA247 does not receive or hold the money, and cannot refund it.
- Transfer the exact amount. A wrong amount is a common reason for a payment to be rejected.
- Bank transfers can sometimes take a little while to show up. If the seller has not confirmed yet, they will check and reply. You will get an email when they confirm or reject.

## Delivery and pickup

- Each seller sets their own delivery fee, which depends on whether you are in their state, and their own delivery time.
- If you choose pickup at checkout, the seller will tell you where and when to collect.
- After you place an order, you can message the seller on WhatsApp from your order confirmation page, where they have given us a number.

## Confirming receipt

When the seller marks your order as sent out, we ask you to confirm when it arrives. If it has not arrived or something is wrong, report a problem instead. If you do not respond, we send a reminder after a few days and mark the order as received after about a week.

## If something goes wrong

- **The seller has not confirmed my payment.** Wait for their reply first. If they have not answered after three days, you can report it to us from your order page. If their store has been suspended, you can report it straight away.
- **The order is wrong, damaged or never came.** Report a problem within five days of placing the order. The seller then has seven days to put it right with you, and if they do not, our team reviews it.
- **Refunds.** Because you paid the seller directly, any refund comes from the seller. We can follow up with them, record a decision, and take action against their store, but we cannot move the money ourselves.

You can report a problem any time from the Report a Problem page. You will need your order reference and the email address you used.
