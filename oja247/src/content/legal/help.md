Need help with an order, your store or your account? Email support@oja247.store and include your order reference if you have one. We reply as soon as we can.

## For shoppers

### How do I pay?
You pay the seller's own bank account by bank transfer. At checkout you see their bank details and the exact amount, then upload your receipt. See [Delivery & Payment Information](/delivery-information) for the full steps.

### The seller has not confirmed my payment yet.
Sellers check their own bank, so it can take a little while. If they have not answered after three days, you can report it to us from your order page.

### Something is wrong with my order.
Use [Report a Problem](/report-problem) within five days of placing the order. You will need your order reference and the email address you used.

### Can OJA247 refund me?
No. You pay the seller directly, so refunds come from the seller. We can follow up with them and take action against their store.

## For sellers

### How do I get paid?
Customers pay your own bank account directly. When an order comes in, check your bank and tap Payment received, or Reject with a reason. See the [Seller Terms](/vendor-terms).

### How do I change my bank details?
Open the Bank & Verification tab in your dashboard. You will be asked for your password, and we will email you to confirm the change.

### My store is hidden.
This usually means your subscription has ended or your account needs a review. Check your dashboard and your email, or contact us.
