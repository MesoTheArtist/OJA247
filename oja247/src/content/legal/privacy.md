This policy explains what personal information OJA247 collects, why, who sees it, and the choices you have. It covers shoppers, sellers and marketers.

## 1. What we collect

- **When you order:** your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, the items you ordered, and the payment receipt you upload (which may show your name, your bank, and part of your account details).
- **If you create an account:** your name, email address and a password (stored in a protected form, never in plain text), or your Google sign-in details if you use that option.
- **If you are a seller:** your business details, your contact and WhatsApp numbers, the bank account customers pay into, and the documents we use to verify you, such as an ID number, a selfie and business registration papers. We also hold your subscription payments and the orders customers place with you.
- **If you are a marketer:** your contact details, referral activity and the bank details we pay you to.
- **Automatically:** basic technical information needed to run and protect the platform, such as your device and browser type and your IP address, and information stored in your browser (for example your cart and login) so the site works.
- **If you subscribe to our newsletter:** your email address.

## 2. Why we use it

- To process your orders and pass them to the right seller.
- To email you about your orders, payments, disputes and account, and to remind sellers to respond.
- To verify sellers and keep the platform safe, including preventing fraud and fake receipts.
- To handle complaints and disputes, and to follow the law.
- To run, improve and support OJA247.

We use your email for marketing only if you have signed up for it, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

## 3. Who sees it

- **The seller you order from** sees your name, contact details, delivery address, the items, and your receipt, so that they can confirm payment and deliver.
- **Our team** can see order and dispute information when it is needed to review a problem or keep the platform safe.
- **Service providers** that help us run OJA247, who only handle data to provide their service to us. They include file storage for images and receipts, our payment partner for subscriptions and marketer payments, email delivery, an identity-check provider for seller verification, and hosting and database providers.
- **Authorities,** if the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Receipts and documents

Receipts and verification documents are stored privately. They are not public, and links to them are temporary and given only to the people who need them, such as the seller of that order and our team.

## 5. How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as we need it for the purposes above, including handling disputes and meeting our legal and accounting obligations, and then delete it or remove what identifies you.

## 6. Keeping it safe

We use sensible technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including protected passwords, private storage for sensitive files, and access limits. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you can object to how we use it, subject to what we must keep by law. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time. Email support@oja247.store and we will respond.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy and will show the date of the latest version above. For important changes we will also tell you by email or on the site.

## 9. Contact

Questions about your information: support@oja247.store.
