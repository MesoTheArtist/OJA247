These rules apply to everything sold through an OJA247 store. They are part of the Seller Terms. The list is not complete: if something would be illegal, dangerous, fraudulent or harmful to customers, it does not belong on OJA247, even if it is not named here.

## 1. Not allowed at all

### Illegal goods and services
- Illegal drugs and drug-making equipment, and anything sold to help use them.
- Stolen goods, or anything you do not have the right to sell.
- Counterfeit, fake or "super copy" goods, including fake designer brands, and pirated films, music, books or software.
- Forged or fake documents, certificates, IDs, stamps and currency.
- Unlicensed gambling, betting or lottery services.

### Weapons and dangerous items
- Firearms, ammunition and parts, explosives, and anything designed to injure people.
- Military or police uniforms, badges and equipment.
- Hazardous or toxic chemicals, fireworks, and any product that has been recalled or declared unsafe.

### Health and body
- Prescription-only medicines, and any medicine, food, cosmetic or supplement that is banned or not properly registered.
- Skin-lightening or other products containing banned substances.
- Human organs, blood and body parts.
- Fake medical claims, such as "cures" for serious diseases.

### Wildlife
- Endangered or protected species, and anything made from them, such as ivory, pangolin scales or protected bushmeat.

### Adult content
- Sexually explicit material or services, and escort or similar services.

### Money, accounts and data
- Investments, loans, "money doubling", Ponzi, get-rich-quick or pyramid schemes, and forex or crypto "signals" that promise profit.
- Selling or renting bank accounts, SIM cards registered to someone else, social media accounts, or identity documents.
- Stolen or leaked data, hacking tools, and services that help someone cheat or defraud others.

### Hate and harm
- Items or content that promote violence, terrorism, hatred or abuse of any group.

## 2. Not allowed because of how you sell

- Listing something you do not have, or taking payment for goods you do not intend to send.
- Bait-and-switch: showing one item and sending another, or changing the price after the customer has paid.
- Misleading photos or descriptions, including pretending used goods are new.
- Fake reviews, paying for reviews, or asking customers to review dishonestly.
- Asking customers to pay anywhere other than the bank account shown at checkout, or to send money "to release" an order.

## 3. Allowed only if you meet the conditions

- **Food, drinks, drugs, cosmetics and supplements** that need NAFDAC registration: include the valid registration number in the listing, and keep products in date.
- **Phones, laptops and other electronics:** say clearly if they are used or refurbished, and be ready to show proof of ownership.
- **Branded goods:** only genuine ones, and say where they came from if a customer asks.
- **Perishable foods:** only if you can deliver them safely and quickly.
- **Live animals:** only where it is legal and you can look after them properly until delivery.

## 4. What happens if these rules are broken

We may remove a listing, hide your store, suspend your account, hold back a bank change for review, and report illegal activity to the authorities. Repeated or serious breaches can mean removal from OJA247. Customers who lost money through a prohibited sale can report the order from the Report a Problem page.

## 5. Report a listing

If you see something on OJA247 that breaks these rules, email support@oja247.store with the store or product link and we will look at it.
